# GRC103-Risk-Monitoring-and-Reporting-Lab

Overview
This project demonstrates the application of risk monitoring, dashboard reporting, risk analysis, and management reporting principles using a fictional cybersecurity dataset.

The objective of the exercise was to calculate and analyse key risk indicators (KRIs), identify emerging risk trends, develop a risk register, create an action and assurance plan, and prepare an executive management report.

Objectives
Monitor cybersecurity risk indicators
Calculate key risk metrics
Analyse risk trends across multiple reporting periods
Develop a risk register
Create action and assurance plans
Produce management-level reporting
Present findings through a dashboard

Monitoring Coverage
Timely Critical Alert Review

Key Findings
Monitoring Coverage
Monitoring coverage declined from 98% to 90%, resulting in 12 endpoints not being actively monitored.

Critical Alert Review
Timely review of critical alerts fell from 95% to 70%, increasing the likelihood of delayed response to security incidents.

Restore Success
Restore-test success declined from 100% to 80%, raising concerns regarding recovery readiness.

Risk Register Summary
The risk assessment identified the following primary risks:

Endpoint monitoring gaps
Delayed review of critical alerts
Backup and recovery weaknesses
Overdue risk treatment actions
Executive Recommendations
Improve endpoint monitoring coverage
Enhance alert triage and escalation procedures
Strengthen backup validation and recovery testing
Reduce overdue risk treatment actions through management oversight
